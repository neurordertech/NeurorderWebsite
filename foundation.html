OUR PURPOSE

Progress should reach people.

iNtsikelelo Foundation is NEURORDER's social-impact initiative. It exists to connect the company's technology, research capacity and partnerships with practical community needs.

Our approach is not built around technology for technology's sake. We look for places where access, information, education and collaboration can create meaningful opportunities for people.

The Foundation also provides a structure through which NEURORDER can collaborate responsibly with community organisations, institutions and other independent partners while keeping social impact distinct from commercial activity.

WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Local needs. Practical action.

The Foundation develops and supports initiatives where knowledge, technology and community participation can work together.

01 · COMMUNITY

Community Support

Supporting practical community initiatives and building relationships with organisations already working directly with people.

02 · EDUCATION

Knowledge & Education

Creating opportunities for young people and communities to access useful knowledge, learning resources and emerging digital tools.

03 · HEALTH

Health Knowledge

Exploring responsible ways for trusted institutions and communities to collaborate around accessible health knowledge and awareness.

04 · OPPORTUNITY

Access & Opportunity

Helping connect people with programmes, information, resources and opportunities that might otherwise remain difficult to find.

FOUNDATION INITIATIVE

Community Health Knowledge Initiative.

CHKI is an emerging collaboration framework under iNtsikelelo Foundation designed to connect communities with trusted health knowledge and participating organisations. Partners remain independent while NOUS provides a digital environment through which information, resources and collaboration can be organised.

Independent partners Participating organisations retain their own identity, responsibilities and professional independence.
Trusted knowledge The initiative is designed around responsible access to useful, understandable community information.
Powered through NOUS NOUS provides the developing digital environment for collaboration, resources and community access.

RESPONSIBLE IMPACT

Built with accountability.

Community work requires more than good intentions. iNtsikelelo Foundation is being developed with defined governance, documentation, partner responsibilities and clear separation between NEURORDER's commercial operations and its social initiatives.

As programmes develop, we aim to measure what was delivered, who participated and whether the work created meaningful value rather than relying only on visibility or publicity.
