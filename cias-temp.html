CIAS is NEURORDER's research into whether alternative economic activity and behavioural indicators can help describe financial reliability where traditional credit records are incomplete, limited or unavailable.

Access status

Locked for legalisation and research.

The scoring environment is not currently open for public use. CIAS remains under research, methodology development, legal review, external moderation and validation before any broader deployment is considered.

The research objective

What are we trying to understand?

CIAS is not designed to simply create another traditional credit score. The research asks whether patterns of activity, consistency, economic volume, stability and other observable financial behaviours may provide useful context when a person's financial life is not fully represented by conventional credit records.

01 · Visibility

Understand financial activity that is usually unseen.

Explore whether recurring economic behaviour can provide structured information about people who participate in informal or under-documented economic activity.

02 · Methodology

Test whether simple rules can produce useful signals.

Examine whether transparent rule-based scoring can provide understandable financial indicators before more advanced modelling is considered.

03 · Inclusion

Explore more inclusive financial intelligence.

Study whether alternative financial context may help reduce information gaps without replacing responsible lending, legal assessment or human judgement.

Research development

Building the system and the methodology.

The current work brings together systems analysis and research methodology. The goal is to ensure that the technical scoring environment and the research questions develop alongside one another rather than allowing the software to move ahead of the evidence.
